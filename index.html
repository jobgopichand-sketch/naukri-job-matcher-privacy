Privacy Policy Naukri Job Matcher Last updated: October 3, 2026 Naukri Job Matcher is a Chrome extension that helps users identify job listings on Naukri that match their specified job requirements. Information Processed The extension reads job listing content displayed on Naukri pages, including job titles and listing text, to compare the listings with the user's search criteria. How Information Is Used The information is used solely to identify and highlight job listings that match the user's specified search criteria. Local Processing Job listing information is processed locally in the user's browser. The extension does not transmit job listing content to an external server. Personal Information The extension does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, passwords, financial information, or other personal information. Data Sharing The extension does not sell or share information processed by the extension with third parties. Data Retention The extension does not maintain a remote database of job listing information or user information. Changes to This Privacy Policy This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality or data practices of the extension change. Contact If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the extension developer through the contact information provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
